RC PILOTS: Manage your fleet, log your flights, and track your batteries anywhere. Effortlessly.

FlightLog is the ultimate digital logbook for heli, plane, and drone pilots. Whether you prefer quick manual entries at the field or deep OpenTX/EdgeTX telemetry imports, FlightLog works the way you do. Why FlightLog

FlightLog lets you note down an event in seconds so you can get straight back to your next pack. You never have to worry about missing a detail or choosing between quick manual notes and raw telemetry. FlightLog captures everything, beautifully combined — getting out of your way at the field and giving you incredible insights right in your pocket.

Key Features

STREAMLINED & SIMPLE

  • Fast Setup — Everything you need, nothing you don't. We skipped the endless menus so you can add gear and get back to flying.
  • Smart Navigation — Search for a tag or apply a filter, and the app remembers it as you jump between screens.
  • Built for the Field — Designed by pilots with a clean layout, smart default values, and an interface that stays out of your way.

FAST MANUAL LOGGING

  • Smart Proposals — FlightLog suggests your next battery, location, and flight time.
  • Custom Tags — Define your own tags to categorize flights exactly how you think about them.
  • Voice Entry — Hands-free flight dictation. Just speak your notes after landing.

FLEET & BATTERIES

  • Complete Tracking — Manage your aircraft configurations, battery cycles, and hardware history.
  • Compatibility Guards — Smart safety checks ensure you never accidentally log a 4S battery on a 6S aircraft.

RICH ANALYTICS

  • Fleet Dashboards — View overall activity tiles, monthly flight trends, and battery usage at a glance.
  • Telemetry Deep-Dive — Chart your imported data per-flight, including head speed, voltage sag, current draw, and ESC temperature.

TELEMETRY & MERGE

  • OpenTX / EdgeTX Import — Drop in your radio logs in batches. The app instantly adapts to whatever sensors your specific aircraft reports.
  • Fuzzy Merge Engine — Log a flight by hand at the field, then import its telemetry later. FlightLog automatically finds the match and merges them into one perfect record. No duplicates.

YOUR DATA, EVERYWHERE

  • Offline-First & Multi-Device — Use every feature out at the field with zero signal. Once you have a connection, your logbook syncs seamlessly across all your devices.
  • True Ownership — Export your entire logbook at any time to keep your own personal backups or spreadsheets.
  • Safe Deletion — A 30-day recoverable Trash protects against accidents. If you ever leave, deleting your account wipes your cloud data completely.

Who It's For

Built for RC hobbyists and flying clubs who want their logbook to be as serious about their data as they are about their aircraft. Free to use, with no ads and no spam.

Privacy — By Default, Not By Policy

Just like at the flight line, you are the pilot in command of your data. FlightLog only asks for Internet (to sync across devices) and Microphone (only if you opt-in to voice logging). Silent tracking and Voice AI are entirely disabled by default. We don't harvest your data, and we only store what is absolutely necessary to keep your logbook running: your email, name, and your flight logs. Full details in the privacy policy.

FlightLog Privacy Policy

High-performance, offline-first RC flight management and telemetry analysis.

Effective date: July 18, 2026

FlightLog ("the app," "we," "us") is developed and operated by an independent developer, contactable at riedlking@gmail.com. This policy explains what data FlightLog collects, why, and how you can control or remove it.

Information We Collect

FlightLog is offline-first: your flight logs and fleet data live on your device first and sync to your own private cloud account for backup and multi-device access. We collect:

  • Account information — your email address and pilot name, collected at sign-up to create and identify your account, and to gate access via manual approval.
  • Pilot profile details (optional) — your RC flying field and AMA/FAA registration number, if you choose to add them to your profile.
  • Flight and fleet data — telemetry logs (RPM, voltage, current, and related flight metrics), and the aircraft and battery profiles you create.
  • Voice audio (optional, off by default) — if you enable hands-free logging, your speech is sent to Google Gemini AI to extract flight details for you to review before saving. Audio is processed in real time and is never stored or logged by FlightLog. You are asked to consent the first time you use the microphone, and can withdraw consent anytime in Configuration.
  • Crash diagnostics (optional, off by default) — if you enable Crash Reporting in Configuration, Firebase Crashlytics sends anonymous crash reports (stack traces, device model, OS version) to help us fix bugs. This never includes your name, email, or flight data.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the app's core functionality: logging, syncing, and analyzing your flights.
  • To operate the manual-approval access model — an administrator reviews new accounts before granting access.
  • To diagnose and fix crashes, if you've opted in to crash reporting.
  • To extract structured flight details from your voice, if you've opted in to voice entry.

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell your data to third parties.

Administrative Transparency

For account approval, your email and pilot name are visible to the app administrator during account creation and account deletion. FlightLog is a small, manually-approved app (typically used by RC flying clubs), so this administrative step is part of how access is granted.

Third-Party Services

FlightLog is built on Firebase (Google) for authentication, cloud storage/sync (Firestore), and optional crash reporting (Crashlytics). If you use voice entry, your speech is additionally sent to Google Gemini AI (via Firebase AI Logic) solely to extract flight details from what you said. These providers process data under their own privacy terms as Google's infrastructure; FlightLog does not share your data with any other third party.

Data Retention & Deletion

  • Trash & automatic purge — deleted flights, aircraft, and batteries move to a Trash area and are automatically and permanently purged after 30 days.
  • Data portability — you can export a complete JSON backup of your account, or a CSV of your flight logs, at any time from Configuration.
  • Account deletion — you can permanently delete your account and all associated cloud data from the Profile screen. This requires typing a confirmation phrase and cannot be undone.

Data Security

Your data is stored in Google Firestore, secured by server-side access rules that restrict every account to its own data, gated by a manual-approval flag that only an administrator can set. Local on-device data is not encrypted at rest beyond the operating system's own protections; the app does not support Android's automatic backup feature (allowBackup="false"), so no local app data is included in device-to-device or cloud OS backups.

Children's Privacy

FlightLog is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and, for material changes, notify users in-app.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to riedlking@gmail.com.
